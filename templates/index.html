⚡ Steel Plant Energy Consumption Dashboard

Welcome! This app was built for Week 3 of the Data Analysis & Business Understanding internship. It serves a machine-learning pipeline trained on the Steel Industry Energy Consumption dataset (UCI, 35,040 readings from 2018).

📊 Dashboard — key EDA visualizations: energy by hour, energy by load type, correlation heatmap, and the PCA variance curve.
🔮 Prediction — enter the plant's operating parameters and get a real-time energy consumption prediction (kWh) from the deployed pipeline (StandardScaler → PCA (10 components) → Random Forest).

Model test performance: RMSE 5.07 kWh · R² 0.977
